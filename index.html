Australia's International Tourism

Who visits, where they go, and what they spend.

International tourism shapes Australia's economy, cities, and communities. In 2025, Australia welcomed 8.9 million international visitors who spent $39.2 billion across the country. This visualisation tells that story — from the countries people come from, to the states they visit, and the value they bring.

The Big Picture

Australia's international tourism industry reached a major milestone in 2025. Visitor numbers and spending both hit record highs, driven by strong growth from Asia, Europe and the Americas.

Who's Coming?

Australia draws visitors from across the globe. The source country shapes how long visitors stay, why they come, and how much they spend.

New Zealand sends the most visitors, while China is the highest-value market.

Bars show total visitor arrivals in 2025, with spend information for each country.

The UK climbed two places in 2025 while Korea dropped out of the top rankings — showing how source markets shift year to year.

Each line connects a country's 2024 rank on the left to its 2025 rank on the right. Rising lines mean a country moved up in arrivals ranking.

Most visitors come for holidays or to visit friends and relatives.

The chart shows the share of visitors by travel purpose in 2025.

Most visitors stay between one and two weeks.

Visitor stays are grouped by trip duration in 2025.

Where Do They Go?

International visitors are not evenly spread across Australia. NSW and VIC dominate, but the share going to other states has shifted over the past decade.

NSW receives the most international visitors in 2025.

Darker shading shows states with more international visitor arrivals.

Visitor arrivals increased across all states in 2025.

The chart compares state arrivals in 2024 and 2025. Darker dots show 2025.

NSW and VIC stayed at the top, while smaller states shifted positions.

Lower ranks mean more visitor arrivals. Lines show how each state's ranking changed over time.

What Do They Spend?

Visitor spending reveals which markets bring the most economic value. The total amount spent and the spend per trip tell very different stories about visitor value.

Holiday arrivals dominate in volume but Education visitors spend significantly more per trip.

Arrivals by purpose are compared across 2015, 2019 and 2025. Each group of bars shows how the mix has shifted over the decade.

Visitor spend has grown faster than arrivals — each visitor is worth more to Australia today than in 2015.

Bars show total annual arrivals and the line shows total visitor spend. Both are shown from 2015 to 2025.

NSW consistently leads all states in arrivals across every year from 2015 to 2025.

Each cell shows arrivals for one state in one year. Darker cells mean more visitors that year.

Chinese visitors spend over $11,000 per trip — nearly six times more than New Zealanders.

Each bubble is one country. Position shows arrivals and spend per trip. Bubble size shows total spend.
